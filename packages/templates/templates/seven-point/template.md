Seven-point structure picks seven moments that hold a story up and links them in order. Many writers plan it backwards: decide the resolution first, then choose a hook that sits as far from it as possible, and fill in the points between.

Each beat becomes one chapter with one outline scene. The scene summary says what the beat is for, and the scene text is a one-line prompt to write from. Delete the prompt as soon as you have written your own words.
