## About this structure

The hero's journey describes a pattern found in many adventure stories: a hero leaves an ordinary life, meets a challenge that tests everything, and comes home with something worth sharing. Departure, initiation and return are the three movements; the twelve stages are the steps inside them.

Based on the twelve-stage hero's journey that Christopher Vogler adapted from Joseph Campbell's work on myth.

## How to use it

1. Read through the beats below and note, in your own words, what each one will be in this book.
2. Open each outline scene, replace the prompt with your own draft, and change its status as you go.
3. Add scenes to a chapter, or split and merge chapters, whenever the story asks for it. The structure is a guide, not a rule.

## The beats

### 1. Ordinary World

Show the hero's everyday life and what it lacks.

### 2. Call to Adventure

A problem or opportunity arrives.

### 3. Refusal of the Call

The hero hesitates.

### 4. Meeting the Mentor

The hero finds guidance, a gift or courage.

### 5. Crossing the Threshold

The hero commits and enters the new world.

### 6. Tests, Allies and Enemies

The hero learns the rules of the new world.

### 7. Approach to the Inmost Cave

The hero prepares for the greatest challenge.

### 8. The Ordeal

The hero faces the greatest crisis yet.

### 9. Reward

The hero survives and claims something.

### 10. The Road Back

The hero chooses to go home, and trouble follows.

### 11. Resurrection

One last test shows who the hero has become.

### 12. Return with the Elixir

The hero comes home with something that helps others.
