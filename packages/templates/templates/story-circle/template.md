The story circle is a loop. A character starts somewhere familiar, wants something, goes somewhere unfamiliar, pays for what they find, and returns different. The top half of the circle is the world they know; the bottom half is the world they do not.

Each beat becomes one chapter with one outline scene. The scene summary says what the beat is for, and the scene text is a one-line prompt to write from. Delete the prompt as soon as you have written your own words.
