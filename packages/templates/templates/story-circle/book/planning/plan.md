## About this structure

The story circle is a loop. A character starts somewhere familiar, wants something, goes somewhere unfamiliar, pays for what they find, and returns different. The top half of the circle is the world they know; the bottom half is the world they do not.

Based on the eight-step story circle popularised by Dan Harmon.

## How to use it

1. Read through the beats below and note, in your own words, what each one will be in this book.
2. Open each outline scene, replace the prompt with your own draft, and change its status as you go.
3. Add scenes to a chapter, or split and merge chapters, whenever the story asks for it. The structure is a guide, not a rule.

## The beats

### 1. You

Show who the protagonist is and the everyday world they know.

### 2. Need

Something is missing, and the protagonist starts to feel it.

### 3. Go

The protagonist steps out of the familiar world.

### 4. Search

The protagonist struggles to cope in the new world.

### 5. Find

The protagonist gets what they were after.

### 6. Take

The protagonist pays a heavy price for it.

### 7. Return

The protagonist heads back toward the familiar world.

### 8. Change

The protagonist, and their old world, are different now.
